A language model on an ESP32-S3 An $8 chip, no internet. The board continues your story token by token, in real time. Source on GitHub.

1. Flash the board (once)

Needs an ESP32-S3 with 16 MB flash and 8 MB octal PSRAM (for example ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 N16R8), a USB cable and Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing erases the board.

Your browser cannot flash boards. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Flashing needs HTTPS.

Designed for bytes per token: 0.63 bits/byte on TinyStories, ~80-95 tokens/s.

The slvDev/esp32-ai TinyStories model (MIT) on our engine: ~87 tokens/s vs 10.6 on its own firmware.

After flashing, close the flasher window, then press Connect board below.
